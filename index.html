The Roles of Diffusion Models in Medical Image Segmentation: A Survey

Abstract

Diffusion models support medical image segmentation through diverse functional roles, yet existing surveys often emphasize technical characteristics rather than how these models contribute to segmentation. This survey reviews more than 130 studies through a role-oriented taxonomy covering direct generative segmentation, prediction refinement and correction, representation learning and feature enhancement, and data synthesis and augmentation. We compare modeling strategies and key design choices, summarize applications, datasets, and evaluation practices, and examine gaps in assessing predictive uncertainty, sampling stability, conditional consistency, and synthetic-data utility. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future directions for efficient, generalizable, and reliable diffusion-based segmentation.
